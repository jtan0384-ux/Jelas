What Jelas does about it

Three things, in the order a case moves through them.

Jelas explains the restriction.

A plain-language reason at the moment access is withheld, built from the institution's own systems. A first-party funding rule becomes "your account needs one deposit from an account in your own name".

Then it resolves it.

A self-serve route back in for the failures that generate the most support contacts: an unfunded account, a new device, a code that never arrived.

And it keeps the record.

Every case stores what was restricted, why, what the customer was told, and how long it took. A defensible file for complaint handling and conduct reporting.

Jelas never makes or overrides the risk decision.It handles what happens next.

Four things it does

Each one maps to a failure that appears in the coded evidence register.

01

Explained restrictions

The customer sees a plain-language reason and an expected resolution window at the moment of restriction. No jargon reaches them: an AML velocity flag becomes "we need to confirm a recent transfer".

02

Self-serve recovery

Guided routes for an unfunded new account, a changed device, a lapsed number and a code that never arrived. Identity checks step up in proportion to the risk of the case.

03

False-positive triage

Each restriction is scored on how likely it is to be a legitimate customer caught by a correct rule. Low-risk cases route to expedited review without touching the AML decision.

04

Conduct audit trail

Every case records what was restricted, why, what the customer was told, and how long resolution took — a defensible file for complaint handling and conduct reporting.

No control is removed. No decision is overridden.No compliance re-approval is triggered.

This prototype is a working click-through built as static HTML, CSS and a small JavaScript router. A production build would use a React frontend, a Python service layer reading fraud and KYC systems over REST APIs, and an append-only case store. No customer data is processed here and no live system is connected.

How we compete

Three reasons an institution buys this rather than extending an existing contract.

01

Detection vendors are paid to flag risk

CTOS Digital, Experian, GBG, Innov8tif and Tookitaki all compete on finding problems. None sells the resolution of the access denial their systems produce.

We integrate, we do not replace

Built at Monash University Malaysia.

Jelas began as an individual project in BFM 3130: 71 public reviews of Malaysian fintech apps, coded one by one. Twenty-six described the same failure — a customer locked out of their own money, with no explanation and no way back. The group took the idea on, surveyed [N] Malaysians to find out how common it really is, and built the prototype you have just used.

The name

Jelas means clear.That is the whole job, in one word.

Why we took it

It is the word you reach for when something finally makes sense — when an explanation lands and the confusion lifts. Nobody has ever said jelas about a reason code.

Because that is how banks speak. Risk scores, velocity flags, verification states. And when one of them fires, the person on the other end gets four words: transfer is unavailable. No cause, no timeline, no way forward.

So we named the company after the thing we owe that person. Every screen has to pass one test: could someone read this while angry, confused and standing at a counter — and know exactly what happened and what to do next?

If the answer is no, it is not finished.
